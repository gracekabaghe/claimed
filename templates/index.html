Questions you probably have

What even is a ChatGPT Business Profile?

On November 10, 2026, OpenAI launched "Businesses in ChatGPT" — a way for companies to claim a verified profile inside ChatGPT, the AI used by 1.2 billion people every week. When someone asks ChatGPT "who's the best dentist near me?" or "compare these two project management tools," ChatGPT now shows business profiles with descriptions, categories, services, and links. If your business isn't claimed, ChatGPT either shows nothing about you — or worse, shows outdated or incorrect information scraped from the web.

How is this different from Google Business Profile?

Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business) launched in 2004 and became the primary way local businesses appeared in Google Search and Maps. It's now a mature, crowded platform — most businesses already have one, and standing out is expensive. ChatGPT Business Profile is the same concept, but it's three days old. The businesses that claim early get the same first-mover advantage that GBP early claimers got in 2004: visibility before the competition arrives, better positioning as the algorithm learns, and a head start on Business Agents (AI-powered customer service inside ChatGPT) before they become mainstream.

Why should I care about appearing in ChatGPT specifically?

Because 1.2 billion people use ChatGPT weekly — and a growing share of them use it instead of Google for product research, comparisons, and local recommendations. ChatGPT doesn't show ads (yet). It shows answers. When your business is the answer to "what's the best [your category] in [your city]," you win the customer without competing for ad space. Early data from OpenAI shows that verified business profiles with complete information and Business Agents appear in significantly more model responses.

Is this available outside the US?

Currently, ChatGPT Business Profiles are only available to US-based businesses. OpenAI has confirmed international expansion is coming, but hasn't announced specific dates. You can run the free readiness scan now — it'll tell you your eligibility path so you're ready the moment it launches in your country.

Can I claim my business without Claimed?

Yes — OpenAI's Business Hub is free to use directly. But the claiming process involves multiple verification steps (Shopify Connect, domain verification, Persona identity check), profile optimization decisions that impact your visibility, and ongoing monitoring that most business owners don't have time for.
