# Product: Claimed

## Platform
Web (Flask + SQLite), mobile-responsive PWA.

## Users
Small business owners and digital agencies. They've heard that ChatGPT now shows business profiles (like Google Business Profiles) and they want their business to appear. They don't know how. They are time-poor, not technical, and willing to pay $29-199/month for a tool that solves this.

Secondary: digital agencies managing 10-200 client profiles. They need bulk management, white-label reports, and an API.

## Product Purpose
Claimed is the first self-service platform that helps businesses claim, optimize, and monitor their presence on ChatGPT. Fill a form, get a readiness report, follow the guided verification wizard, and track your visibility — all without reading OpenAI's documentation.

Success metric: a business owner goes from "ChatGPT has business profiles?" to "my business is verified and appearing in ChatGPT results" in under 10 minutes.

## Positioning
"The Google Business Profile for the AI era — claim yours before your competitor does."

Claimed is the tool that makes ChatGPT Business Profiles accessible to every business, not just the ones with SEO agencies. It's BrightLocal for ChatGPT: guided, automated, and priced for small business.

## Operating Context
Users land on the site from Google ("chatgpt business profile tool"), Facebook ads ("Get your business on ChatGPT"), or agency referrals. They see a readiness checker immediately — enter your business URL, get a scored report. The report becomes the lead magnet. From there, guided onboarding: verification, profile optimization, insights dashboard.

## Capabilities
- Business readiness scanner (free, no signup)
- Guided verification wizard (phone/email/domain/Shopify routing)
- AI-powered profile optimizer (description, categories, product info)
- ChatGPT visibility tracker (mentions, clicks, keyword appearance)
- Competitor intelligence (who's claimed, how they compare)
- Multi-location dashboard (agency tier)
- Business Agent builder (when OpenAI rolls that out broadly)

## Brand Commitments
Expert, helpful, trustworthy. Claimed speaks with the confidence of someone who understands both SEO and AI. The tone is direct ("Here's what to do"), specific ("Your business is eligible for domain verification"), and never hype-y. We don't say "AI-powered revolution." We say "ChatGPT shows your business to 1.2 billion people. Here's how to make sure it shows the right thing."

Three-word personality: **knowledgeable, direct, reassuring**.

Specifically avoid:
- Generic AI-tool marketing (neon, gradients, glassmorphism, purple)
- SaaS landing-page clichés (hero metrics, identical feature cards, sparklines)
- Hedging language ("might," "could," "consider")
- Over-promising ("dominate ChatGPT search")
- Dark mode as default (our users are business owners, not developers)
